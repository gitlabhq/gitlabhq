If you used GitLab Credits, you had no way to check your own usage.
Only group Owners and administrators could open the GitLab Credits dashboard,
so you had to ask someone with access.

You can now see your own usage in **User settings > GitLab Credits**. The page
shows your total usage, daily average, and peak day, and you can filter by
product or select a date range. If you belong to more than one group with
GitLab Credits, you can switch between them.
