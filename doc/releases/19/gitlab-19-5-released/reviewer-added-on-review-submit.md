When you submit a review on a merge request you are not yet a reviewer of, with **Comment**,
**Approve**, or **Request changes**, GitLab now adds you to the **Reviewers** list. The review
drawer tells you beforehand: "You will be added as a reviewer." The Reviewers list and the
people who took part in the review no longer drift apart. This behavior has been available
since GitLab 19.4, when its feature flag was removed.

Thank you to [Michael Fischer](https://gitlab.com/mfischer5) for this contribution ([MR](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab/-/merge_requests/250880))!
