In previous versions of GitLab, Observability was an experiment. Now it's in beta on GitLab.com and GitLab Self-Managed.

With GitLab Observability, you can send traces, metrics, and logs with OpenTelemetry, then explore them next to your code, merge requests, and CI/CD pipelines.
On GitLab.com, each group or personal namespace opts in by selecting **Enable Observability**.
On GitLab Self-Managed, you host the Observability backend and connect it to a group or personal namespace.
Observability is available at no cost during the beta.
